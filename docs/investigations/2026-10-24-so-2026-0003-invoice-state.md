## ⚠️ A CREDENTIAL EXPOSURE I CAUSED, REPORTED RATHER THAN BURIED

The scan above was first written as `SELECT *` over each candidate table. `agent_jobs`
has an `approval_token` column, and the value for job `45bc7682-…fa76e` was therefore
**printed into the session transcript**. CLAUDE.md §7 is unconditional: no credential
value passes through a session, in either direction.

What it authorises, measured rather than assumed:

- `POST /api/v1/agents/approve/{token}` (`agents.py`) takes `token` and `db` — **no auth
  dependency**. The token is the auth.
- `TOKEN_EXPIRY_HOURS = 72`, job started 2026-10-24 03:04 UTC → valid to ~2026-10-27
  03:04 UTC.
- Blast radius is **bounded**: `ar_collections` is in
  `ApprovalGateService.SIMPLE_APPROVAL_TYPES`, so `_process_approve` sets the job
  `approved` then `complete` and commits. No period lock. No financial write. No email —
  the Phase 3b "trigger email sends for approved collection drafts" TODO is still open on
  that path. The realistic harm is that one collections job is marked complete or
  rejected out from under the office.

**Remedy, which does not require a credential to transit a session:** approve or reject
job `45bc7682-…fa76e` through the authenticated dashboard, or let it expire. I did not
and cannot rotate it — that is a production write and handling the value again is the
thing prohibited.

**The restructure, proposed not built.** Two levels:

1. *Done here.* The probe now names its columns. A probe that `SELECT *`s an unfamiliar
   table cannot promise it will not print a secret, so it does not get to.
2. *For James.* Store `agent_jobs.approval_token` **hashed**, with the plaintext existing
   only in the email URL. Then no read of that table — by a probe, a support engineer, a
   log, or a backup — yields a usable credential, and the rule stops depending on anyone
   remembering it. This is CLAUDE.md's own *removal before recognition*: a validated
   field is a hole with a guard on it; an absent one is not a hole. Not built — it is a
   schema change on a financial approval path.
